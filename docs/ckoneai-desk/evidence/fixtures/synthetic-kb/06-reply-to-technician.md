# How to reply to a technician

Open the ticket and write in **Conversations**. Technicians can also leave internal notes that you do not see.

When a ticket is in **Waiting on User**, your reply puts it back in progress and the paused service clock continues. A ticket on **Hold due to Purchase from HO** does not change when you reply; only the technician can resume it.
