# What is CKDesk

CKDesk is Citykart's service desk. Stores and offices raise a **ticket** for a problem or a request (for example an AC that is not cooling). A technician group works the ticket and the person who raised it can follow it, reply, reopen it after it is resolved, and rate the service.

## Who uses it
- **Requesters**: store and office staff who raise tickets.
- **Technicians**: members of a technician group who work the tickets of that group.
- **Managers and administrators**: see tickets across the company and set CKDesk up.

## Where things are
Raise a ticket from **New Request** (the Services page). Follow your tickets under **Requests**.
