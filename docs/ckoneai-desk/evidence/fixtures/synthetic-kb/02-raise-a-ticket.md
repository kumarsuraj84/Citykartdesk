# How to raise a ticket

1. Open **New Request** and choose the service that matches your problem.
2. Choose the **category** (the list is short and depends on the service).
3. Fill in the form. Fields marked as required must be filled before the ticket can be raised. Some fields appear only for certain categories.
4. Add files if you have them. Pictures, PDF and Office files are accepted, up to 25 MB for one file.
5. Press **Submit**. You get a ticket number that looks like CKSD-000123.

## Good to know
- You cannot choose the priority. It follows the category you picked.
- If you press Submit twice by mistake, the second press returns the same ticket.
- You only see the services that are open to your location.
