# How to reopen a ticket

A resolved ticket can be reopened by the person who raised it **within the reopen window** (the administrator sets it; the usual value is 3 days). You must write a **reason**.

1. Open the resolved ticket.
2. Choose **Reopen** and write why the problem is not fixed.

After the window the ticket closes by itself and cannot be reopened; raise a new ticket instead. A reopened ticket gets a fresh resolution time.
