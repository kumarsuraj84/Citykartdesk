# How approvals work

Some tickets need an approval before work continues (for example a purchase). An approval has one or more **steps**. A step names a person, or "any manager".

- While an approval is open the ticket is **Pending Approval**.
- The approver approves or rejects and may write a comment. A rejected approval cancels the ticket; the requester can reopen it within 48 hours.
- You cannot approve your own request.
- Approvers see approvals under **Approvals** in the menu.
