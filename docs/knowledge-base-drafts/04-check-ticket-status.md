# How to check the status of your ticket

Open **Requests** in the menu. Each ticket shows its number, status and the technician group. Open a ticket to see the conversation and the history.

If you ask the assistant, give the ticket number, for example "status of CKSD-000123". The assistant can show only tickets you raised (or, for a technician, tickets assigned to you).
