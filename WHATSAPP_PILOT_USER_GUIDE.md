# CITYKART DESK ON WHATSAPP — PILOT USER GUIDE

Thanks for being part of the WhatsApp pilot for Citykart Desk. This is a short guide to get you started.

## What number do I message?

*(To be filled in once the pilot WhatsApp number is live — your pilot coordinator will share the exact number with you.)*

## Who can use this?

Right now, only a small group of pilot participants — you're one of them because your mobile number is already registered in Citykart Desk with WhatsApp access turned on. This pilot currently only covers **IT Support** and **HR Support** requests.

## How do I start?

Just send **"Hi"** to the pilot number. If your mobile number is recognized, you'll get a welcome message and a list of services to choose from.

## How do I search for an issue?

After picking a Service (e.g. IT Support), you'll be asked to describe your issue in a few words — like "printer issue" or "laptop not starting." The bot will show you a short list of matching issue types; pick the one that's closest to your problem.

If nothing matches well, try describing it differently — a few different words often find a better match.

## What happens after that?

You'll be asked for a few more details specific to that issue type — some services ask for a phone number, some ask for a required photo/attachment, and so on. Just answer each question as it comes; you'll see exactly what's needed at each step.

## How do attachments work?

If a required photo or file is requested, just send it as a normal WhatsApp image or document. It'll be checked automatically (right file type, reasonable size) — if something's wrong with it, you'll be told and asked to send a different one.

## What if I want to stop partway through?

Send **"Cancel"** at any point to stop and discard what you've started. If you want to begin again, just send **"Hi"** — you'll get a clean, fresh start.

If you go quiet mid-conversation and come back later (same day), you'll pick up exactly where you left off — you won't have to re-answer anything you've already answered. If you come back after a full day of inactivity, your old conversation will have expired and you'll start fresh.

## What happens after my ticket is created?

You'll get a confirmation message with your ticket number. From there, your ticket is handled exactly like any ticket raised through the normal Citykart Desk website — same priority rules, same response-time targets, same team.

*(Note: checking ticket status, replying to updates, or reopening a ticket over WhatsApp are not part of this pilot yet — use the normal Citykart Desk website for those for now.)*

## What if my number isn't recognized?

If you message the pilot number and don't get the expected welcome response, it likely means your mobile number in Citykart Desk doesn't exactly match what you're messaging from, or WhatsApp access hasn't been turned on for your account yet. Contact your pilot coordinator to check.

## Questions or issues?

Reach out to your pilot coordinator — see the support/escalation contacts your organization has shared with you for this pilot.
