Dear All,

We are happy to announce that we have created and are now launching our own in-house ticketing solution, “CITYKARTDESK”, or “CKDesk” in short.

CKDesk is built for the way Citykart works, to make raising and following up your requests simple and quick.
WHAT CHANGES FROM NOW
We are closing the Zoho ticketing solution.
No new tickets will be logged on Zoho from now onwards.
All users must log every new ticket on CKDesk.
What about the tickets already open on Zoho?

Nothing is lost. All existing tickets on Zoho will continue to be taken care of by the technicians who are handling them, and they will close all of those tickets as well. You do not need to raise them again.
  Where to go
New tickets Log on CKDesk only
Old tickets (already on Zoho) Handled and closed on Zoho by the technician who owns them
