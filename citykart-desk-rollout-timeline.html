What shipped in the final build pass (14 Sep 2026)

  • Store & OEM master data, with automatic OEM email routing for AC/vendor tickets
  • Ticket reopen workflow — after resolution or after a rejected approval
  • Per-event notification rules (email / in-app / push) + web push notifications
  • Configurable sender email — no code change needed to update it
  • Report Builder: fixed blank descriptions and hidden custom-service fields
  • Category search, one-service-per-category rule, category CSV export
  • 24-hour time everywhere in the app; unused Tags feature removed
  • Full audit pass on SLA timing, approval handling & file-upload safety
